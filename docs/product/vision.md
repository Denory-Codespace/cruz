# Cruz — Product Vision

## Executive Summary
Cruz is a next-generation mobility and ride-hailing platform purpose-built for the Kenyan and broader African market. Starting with zero external capital expenditure, Cruz leverages modern web, mobile, and cloud-free-tier technologies to deliver a fast, reliable, safe, and transparent ride-hailing experience for passengers, drivers, and fleet operators.

## Core Problem Statement
Existing ride-hailing solutions in the region suffer from:
1. **High platform commissions** squeezing driver earnings and driving disengagement.
2. **Opaque fare pricing** causing friction and mistrust between riders and drivers.
3. **Fragmented driver onboarding & safety checks**, creating operational bottlenecks.
4. **Heavy, slow mobile apps** that consume excessive bandwidth and battery on entry-level Android devices.

## The Cruz Solution
- **Lightweight, Modern Frontends**: High-performance Next.js Web/PWA and lean Expo React Native mobile apps.
- **Fair Financial Ledger**: Transparent fare calculation and instant driver ledger transparency.
- **Robust Driver Onboarding & Live Operations**: Centralized management portal for verification, dispatch monitoring, and support resolution.
- **Zero-Cost Foundation**: Engineered to scale from KES 0 budget to millions of trips without premature architectural bloat.

## Strategic Market Rollout
1. **Pilot Phase (Nairobi, Kenya)**: Closed loop validation with 5–10 drivers and 20–50 passengers.
2. **Commercial MVP Launch**: Full passenger and driver web/PWA and mobile rollout with M-Pesa integration.
3. **Expansion Phase**: Boda-boda, delivery, and regional multi-city expansion.
